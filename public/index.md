# TMYTRN LLC

TMYTRN LLC is the web consulting and development practice of Tommy Tran.

## Who

Tommy Tran is a web designer and developer based in New York. He designs and builds Shopify and Next.js sites for DTC and creative brands, with a focus on ecommerce strategy, performance, email flows, SEO, and AI / agentic search.

## Capabilities

- Shopify sites (themes and custom storefronts)
- Next.js sites
- Ecommerce strategy and conversion optimization
- Klaviyo and Mailchimp email flows
- SEO for web search
- AI SEO / agentic search optimization
